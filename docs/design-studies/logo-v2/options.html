Logo study, second pass

Twelve marks that measure, not pattern

The shipped mark is eight dots on a 3 by 3 grid with an empty centre. The idea still holds, but the drawing does not carry it. Eight identical dots read as a pattern block, so the mark sits in the same family as every loader, particle icon, and dot-grid app tile. It says pattern, not measurement, and it spends the centre idea on a shape that no one reads twice. This study replaces the pattern with a device. Every option below is built from one construction idea in the two reference books, and every one keeps the site's meaning: it measures a person, it does not diagnose one.

No mark picked. Click a card to place it in the header.

The rules

Every option follows the same rules, so the set compares cleanly.

One grid: 40 by 40 units, centre at 20, 20, with an 8 unit margin.

One weight: stroke 4 on the grid, with round caps and round joins.

One colour: the mark inherits currentColor; the stylesheet owns the ink.

Two values: the mark must hold at 28 pixels, at 16 pixels, and reversed out of a solid field.

No faces, no heads, no brains, no medical cross. The site makes no clinical claim.

No letters. The wordmark carries the name; the mark carries the idea.

All twelve at 28 pixels, at 16 pixels, and reversed out of the accent.

What the study read

Both books are page scans, so they were read as plates and captions, not as text.

Kuwayama, volume 2 Contents, the essay Trademarks change with the times, and the chapter Design competitions. Plate chapters read in full for geometry: Circles (1) Radial lines, straight lines, curves; Circles (2) Revolution; Circles (3) Revolution, continued; Rectangles; Triangles; Polygons. Read in part: Designs based on human figures, arrows, and signs.
Logo Modernism The contents page, the two essays, and the essay plates that carry captioned works. The catalogue sections that this study uses are Geometric (Angular 58, Basic Forms 72, Circle 82, Cross 106, Dots 110, Lines 120, Square 142, Triangle 150) and Effect (Cut-off 156, Split 162, Grid 178, Reflection 182, Outline 192, Overlay 204, Positive/Negative 212, Rotation 224, White on Black 242). The page numbers are the section starts from the book's contents page.
Not read The remainder of Kuwayama's subject chapters, most of the Logo Modernism catalogue, and all of its Typographic and Profiles sections. No option here depends on those parts. The mark to name pairing is the open naming study, not this one.
