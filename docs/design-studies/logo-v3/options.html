Three directions worth taking further: 01 Temu, 03 Lapisan, and 06 Belah.
Each mark is shown large, at 24 px, at 16 px, and reversed. The Indonesian labels name the concepts, not a new site brand.

Temu01

Two sides. One conversation.

Construction & reference

Figure-ground profiles.

Kuwayama, printed pp. 20-21 (PDF page 13): faces represented by contour and counterform.

Face recognition is a design intention, not a tested user finding. Check resemblance before adoption.

Lipat02

One ribbon turns to show its other side.

Construction & reference

Folded ribbon.

Logo Modernism, printed pp. 84-85 (PDF page 42): a single silhouette articulated by internal channels.

The folded band can also read as an S. It is not tied to a confirmed site name.

Lapisan03

There is more beneath the first answer.

Construction & reference

Open contour.

Kuwayama, printed pp. 108-109 (PDF page 57): nested curves and deliberately interrupted concentric forms.

The innermost contour is the first detail to weaken at favicon size.

Ragam04

Different dimensions, without a fixed type.

Construction & reference

Unequal radial lobes.

Kuwayama, printed pp. 108-109 (PDF page 57): rotational construction; varied here by lobe length rather than repeated identical units.

The floral association is intentional. It is warmer but less specific to psychological tests.

Taut05

Two independent forms, one connected whole.

Construction & reference

Interrupted interlace.

Kuwayama, printed pp. 108-109 (PDF page 57): interwoven bands. Open interruptions replace overprinted crossings.

Interlocking bands are a crowded trademark category. A similarity review is still required.

Belah06

The same person, another perspective.

Construction & reference

Curved division.

Logo Modernism, printed pp. 84-85 (PDF page 42): positive and negative shapes share an outer silhouette.

The organic silhouette can suggest a seed or flame. Avoid claims of treatment or transformation.

Raut07

A person revealed by the space left open.

Construction & reference

Profile as negative space.

Kuwayama, printed pp. 20-21 (PDF page 13): faces constructed from a containing silhouette and a profile-shaped counterform.

The facial reading is more literal than the other options. It does not imply a clinical service.

Pantul08

Recognition through reflection.

Construction & reference

Paired organic bands.

Kuwayama, printed pp. 20-21 (PDF page 13): paired contours and faces; abstracted here into two opposing bands.

The paired curves can read as two S letters. No name is implied.

Mekar09

A fuller picture, opening gradually.

Construction & reference

Graduated fan.

Logo Modernism, printed pp. 84-85 (PDF page 42): directional curved bands; Kuwayama, pp. 98-99 (PDF page 52): radial construction.

The botanical association can resemble wellness brands. This is the softest direction.

Bidang10

A result has depth, not a single label.

Construction & reference

Offset folded planes.

Logo Modernism, printed pp. 84-85 (PDF page 42): contrasting internal planes inside a common envelope; translated here into open angular bands.

The angular band can suggest architecture or software. It is a more technical direction.

Jejak11

An individual path, not a ranking.

Construction & reference

Returning line.

Kuwayama, printed pp. 110-113 (PDF pages 58-59): angular and returning paths; constructed here as two interdependent routes.

The route can read as a monogram. Keep its geometry separate from the eventual wordmark.

Inti12

An outer form with an unexpected interior.

Construction & reference

Nested asymmetric arches.

Kuwayama, printed pp. 20-21 (PDF page 13): enclosing contours; pp. 108-109 (PDF page 57): nested shapes with an offset centre.

The narrow inner counter is the weakest detail at 16 pixels. A dedicated favicon may be needed.
